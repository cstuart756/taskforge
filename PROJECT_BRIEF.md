# TaskForge — Project Brief

## What is it?

TaskForge is a web-based task management application (SaaS) for small teams.
It lets team members create, assign, and track tasks in a simple, fast interface.

## The problem it solves

Small teams (2–15 people) often outgrow spreadsheets and sticky notes but cannot
justify the cost and complexity of enterprise tools like Jira or Asana. They need
something simple, affordable, and focused on the essentials.

## Who uses it?

- **Team member:** creates tasks, updates status, comments.
- **Supervisor:** assigns tasks, monitors progress, reviews completed work.
- **Administrator:** manages users, roles, and team settings.
- **Business owner:** reviews overall team performance and activity.

## Why would someone choose this?

- Simple interface — no training required.
- Affordable monthly subscription per team.
- Focused on small teams, not enterprise bloat.
- Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## How does it make money?

Monthly subscription:
- Free tier: up to 3 users, 50 tasks.
- Pro tier: unlimited users and tasks (£9 per team per month).

## What does a successful launch look like?

- 10 teams actively using TaskForge within 3 months of launch.
- Registration-to-active-use conversion above 40%.
- Monthly churn below 5%.
- Positive feedback from at least 5 users.

## What is out of scope for version 1?

- Advanced analytics and reporting dashboards.
- AI-powered task suggestions.
- Native mobile apps.
- Third-party integrations (Slack, Google Calendar).
- Multi-language support.

These may be considered in future versions.

## Success criteria for the technical build

- Users can register, log in, and log out securely.
- Users can create, edit, delete, and complete tasks.
- Supervisors can assign tasks to team members.
- Administrators can manage users.
- The application works on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- The application deploys reliably to Heroku (EU region).
- Automated tests cover critical user journeys.
- The codebase is documented and maintainable.
